# Sugar Tracker

A prototype that tracks sugar levels and uses health trends to give predictive alerts.

## Why I built it
This one is personal. My dad is 80 and lives in India, and his sugar levels fluctuate.
I wanted peace of mind from the other side of the world.

He records his readings in the app, and I watch the trends and predictions. If something
looks off, I call him and ask him to see his doctor. It helps us catch problems early
and avoid emergency visits, especially when I can't be there to help right away.

## What it does
- Logs sugar readings and shows trends over time
- Uses simple machine learning models (polynomial and incremental regression) to predict upcoming levels
- Alerts when readings are trending toward a range to watch

## Built with
Python, scikit-learn, a lightweight web front end, developed on Replit with AI coding assistants.
